Welcome to The Flyover Bridge Africa

Building Bridges to Inclusion and Hope

Across Africa, children living with disabilities and vulnerable families are too often left without access to education, healthcare, or a place to belong. We build the bridge between where they are and the future they deserve.

  • Safe Shelter

    Providing safe, accessible homes for children with disabilities who have nowhere else to go.

  • Clean Water

    Ensuring accessible water and sanitation facilities that children with mobility challenges can use safely.

  • Inclusive Education

    Building accessible classrooms and training teachers to support children with physical, sensory, and intellectual disabilities.

  • Therapy & Healthcare

    Providing physiotherapy, assistive devices, and essential medical care for children with disabilities.

About Us

Why Choose Us

Give Every Child a Fair Chance to Belong

To empower children with disabilities and vulnerable communities through quality education, healthcare, and medical insurance, creating opportunities for a better future.

The Flyover Bridge Africa Foundation (FBA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to transforming the lives of children with disabilities and vulnerable families across Africa. The foundation serves as a bridge that connects disadvantaged children to quality education, healthcare, medical insurance, leadership development, and life-changing opportunities. Through compassion, partnership, and community empowerment, The Flyover Bridge Africa Foundation works to ensure that every child, regardless of disability or background, has the chance to grow, learn, and become a responsible leader who contributes to the development of Africa. Together, we are building a bridge from vulnerability to hope, dignity, and a brighter future.

  • Inclusive Education for Every Child

  • Therapy, Assistive Devices & Healthcare

  • Safe Shelter & Family Support

  • Community Awareness & Acceptance
