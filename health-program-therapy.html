Free Disability Screening & Therapy Camp 2026

February 23, 2026 Mobile Clinics Across East Africa

Providing free disability screening, therapy sessions, and assistive device fittings for children in underserved communities. Our mobile teams travel to remote areas to ensure every child has access to early intervention services regardless of location.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive disability screenings and assessments
  • Free physiotherapy and occupational therapy sessions
  • Speech therapy evaluations and sessions
  • Assistive device fittings and training
  • Parent education and support groups
  • Follow-up care coordination

Progress

  • 500 screenings completed this season
  • 50 therapists trained and deployed
  • 25 mobile units operational
  • 80% of children referred for ongoing care
